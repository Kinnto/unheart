# Chrome Web Store listing — Unheart

## Name
Unheart — Bulk Unlike for X

## Summary (≤132 chars)
Remove your likes on X in bulk with one click. Turn your likes into ex-likes.

## Description
Unheart clears the likes on your X account — hundreds or thousands — with one click.

• One click: open your Likes and press "Unlike all". Or try 10 first.
• Honest count: a like only counts as removed once it actually stays removed.
• Handles limits for you: Unheart paces itself and automatically waits when X asks it to slow down, then carries on.
• Pause, resume or stop at any time. Progress survives a page reload.
• Private: everything runs in your browser. No account, no servers, nothing collected.

How to use
1. Click the Unheart icon — it opens your Likes on X.
2. Click "Unlike all" (or "try 10 first").
3. Keep the tab visible while it runs — put its window side by side with your other work.

Unheart is not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp. Automated actions are subject to X's terms; use at your own discretion.
